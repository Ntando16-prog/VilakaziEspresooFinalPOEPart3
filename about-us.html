Our Story

More than Coffee. A piece of Soweto

We are Located on the World-famous Vilakazi Street in Soweto, next to Nelson Mandela House, Vilakazi Expresso & Curios was born from a simple idea: great coffee should come with great culture.

We are more than just an expresso shop. we are a meeting place for locals, tourists, and storytellers. While you sip on our 5-star coffee and enjoy fresh muffins, you can browse authentic African curios, art and beadwork made by local artists.

Nestled on the historic Vilakazi Street - the only street in the world to have housed two Nobel Prive winners Vilakazi Espresso & Curios was born from a simple idea: to blend world-class coffee with authentic Soweto culture.

We are not just a coffee shop. We are a gallery, a memory shop and a meeting place. Every cup is brewed with heart, and every curio is curated with soul.

"Brewed with Heart. Curated with Soul."

Our Mission

To serve exceptional coffee while preserving and celebrating the vibrant heritage of Soweto through locally mage curios, art, and stories.

Our Vision

Ethically sourced beans, proudly South African products, and a warm, elegant space where everyone feels at home on Vilakazi Street.

What Makes Us Different

  • Coffee + Culture: Premium expresso paired with african art and sourenirs
  • Location: The heart of Soweto, on Vilakazi Street - the only street in the world with Nobel Peace Prize winners
  • Community: We support local artists and serve the people of Soweto with pride
  • Experience: Warm, welcoming space to relax, shop, and soak in the history
